What if exit wasn't free — but earned through time?

Most tokens are built on instant liquidity. Buy fast, sell faster. Price spikes, insiders exit, holders absorb the loss. The same cycle, endlessly repeated.

ANKA Engine is built on a different premise. What if the smart contract itself limited how fast you could leave? What if supply shrank on every trade? What if there was no admin who could change any of this — ever?

That's not a promise. It's an experiment. Fully on-chain, fully verifiable, fully immutable.

Traditional Token

  • Instant buy and instant sell for everyone
  • No restrictions on exit timing or volume
  • Admin controls: blacklists, mint functions, upgrades
  • High speculation cycles with fast dump potential
  • Liquidity can be pulled by the team at any time
  • Supply can be inflated post-launch

ANKA Engine Model

  • Entry always open — buying is never restricted
  • Exit is rate-limited — daily wallet sell limits enforced by contract
  • Zero admin control — ownership renounced at deployment
  • Supply only goes down — 1% destroyed on every TX
  • Liquidity locked permanently — no dev withdrawal possible
  • Immutable logic — no one can change how this works
Core Mechanics

Three Layers. Every Transaction.

1%
Burn

Permanent Burn

1% of every transaction amount is permanently destroyed — removed from the total supply forever. This continues with every single on-chain interaction until the circulating supply reaches the hard floor of 10,000,000 ANKA. At that point, the burn mechanism stops automatically. Supply becomes fixed. No one can change this. The contract is immutable and ownership is renounced.

Deflationary · Automatic · Unstoppable
2%
Fee

Protocol Fee Distribution

A 2% fee is collected on every transaction and immediately converted to BNB. The split is hard-coded in the contract and cannot be altered:
· 66% routes to the creator wallet for transparent operational funding
· 34% routes to a designated charity wallet
No hidden allocations. No team wallet drains. All wallets are publicly visible on-chain.

Transparent · Hard-Coded · On-Chain
TIME
Exit

Time-Based Exit Mechanism

Unlike every standard token, ANKA Engine does not allow unrestricted instant selling. Wallet-based daily sell limits are enforced by the smart contract. As the circulating supply decreases over time, sell capacity also decreases proportionally. Exit becomes slower as the system matures — intentionally. This mechanic is fully on-chain, immutable, and cannot be disabled by any party.

Rate-Limited · Wallet-Based · Immutable
