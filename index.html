The Thesis

"What happens when exit is not instant — but structured over time?"

Most tokens share a common structure: instant buying, instant selling, and high speculation cycles driven by short-term volatility.

ANKA Engine explores a fundamentally different question. What if liquidity is earned through time rather than granted freely?

By removing instant exit, restructuring supply dynamics, and eliminating all administrative control, ANKA Engine creates a market governed purely by on-chain logic — not human intervention.

There is no admin. No upgrade path. No hidden mechanism. The contract is deployed, renounced, and permanent.

Core Mechanics

Three-Layer Transaction System

01

Burn Mechanism

1% of every transaction is permanently destroyed. Supply decreases with every on-chain interaction until the hard 10,000,000 ANKA cap is reached — at which point the mechanism halts automatically and is locked forever.

1% per TX
02

Protocol Fee

A 2% fee is collected per transaction and converted to BNB. Distribution is hard-coded and fully transparent: 66% funds operations via the creator wallet, 34% routes to a designated charity wallet. No hidden allocations.

2% fee
03

Time-Based Exit

Unlike standard tokens, ANKA does not permit unrestricted instant selling. Wallet-based daily sell limits are enforced by immutable contract logic. Exit is gradual rather than instantaneous — structurally, by design.
