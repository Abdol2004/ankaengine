What if exit wasn't free — but earned through time?

Most tokens are built on instant liquidity. Buy fast, sell faster. Price spikes, insiders exit, holders absorb the loss.

ANKA Engine explores a different structure. What if the smart contract itself rate-limited how fast you could leave? What if supply shrank on every trade? What if no admin could ever change any of this?

That's not a promise. It's an experiment — fully on-chain, fully verifiable, and fully immutable.

As circulating supply decreases over time, sell capacity decreases proportionally — exit becomes slower as the system matures. This mechanic is fully on-chain, immutable, and cannot be disabled by any party.

Introduction

ANKA Engine is a decentralized experimental token system deployed on BNB Smart Chain. It is designed to study how market behavior changes when liquidity is constrained and exit is time-restricted, while maintaining full decentralization and immutability.

There is no admin control, no upgradeability, and no hidden mechanism. The contract is fully on-chain and immutable.

Why ANKA Engine Exists

Most tokens in the crypto market share a common structure:

  • Instant buying and instant selling
  • High speculation cycles
  • Short-term volatility as the primary driver

"What happens when exit is not instant — but structured over time?"

ANKA Engine explores a different approach: a market where exit is not a right but a rate-limited function of the system itself.

Core System Design

ANKA Engine introduces a constrained liquidity model where:

  • Entry is always open — no restriction on buying
  • Exit is rate-limited by smart contract logic
  • Supply continuously decreases over time
  • No external control exists — fully renounced

This creates a time-based market structure instead of instant liquidity trading. The psychological and economic effects of delayed exit systems have not been studied at this scale on-chain before.

Token Mechanism

Each transaction includes a 3-layer mechanism:


  • 1% Burn — permanently removes tokens from circulation, reducing total supply with every interaction
  • 2% Protocol Fee — converted to BNB: 66% to creator wallet, 34% to charity wallet
  • Burn Cap — burn stops automatically at 10,000,000 ANKA and cannot be restarted

Governance & Control

ANKA Engine enforces trustlessness at every level:


  • Ownership renounced — no entity controls the contract post-deployment
  • No admin keys — no private key can modify system behavior
  • No upgrade functions — the contract logic is final, no proxy, no migration
  • No blacklist — no address can be restricted from the system

Objective

  • Liquidity-constrained market behavior under real on-chain conditions
  • Supply reduction dynamics and their effect on price discovery
  • Psychological effects of delayed exit systems on holder behavior
  • On-chain economic experimentation at scale
