How It Works

1%

Burn Mechanism

Every transaction permanently destroys 1% of the amount. Supply shrinks continuously until the 10,000,000 ANKA cap is hit — then burn stops automatically and supply is fixed forever.

2%

Protocol Fee

2% per transaction is converted to BNB on-chain. Split is hard-coded: 66% to creator wallet (operations), 34% to charity wallet. Fully transparent — no hidden allocations.

⏱

Time-Based Exit

Selling is rate-limited by smart contract logic. Wallet-based daily sell limits prevent instant mass exits. Exit is gradual and structured — by immutable design.
